The Final Vision Lock and its Bubble-first correction are the highest authority for JASIM’s product and architecture direction. JASIM is one intelligent conversation driving a universal generative runtime; it must not collapse into domain-specific handlers, a conventional dashboard, a template collection, or an LLM-authoritative executor.

**Why:** Earlier implementation correctly established a general Task/World runtime, but the vision clarifies that Tasks and Worlds are not the product center. Conversation is where the user speaks; Smart Generative Bubbles are the living user-facing surfaces; a World Runtime is optional durable internal life behind a persistent Bubble.

**How to apply:** Before broad implementation, use `docs/JASIM_FINAL_VISION_GAP_ANALYSIS.md` and `docs/JASIM_SMART_BUBBLE_ARCHITECTURE.md` as the Vision Gate. Do not make a World route or renderer the product center, and do not treat a field’s presence in a schema as feature completion. Preserve the distinction between model proposals and durable Runtime truth.
