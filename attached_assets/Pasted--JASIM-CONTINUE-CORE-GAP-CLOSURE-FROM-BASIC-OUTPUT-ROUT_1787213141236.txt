# FINAL PRODUCT PRINCIPLE

ثبت هذه القاعدة:

> Jasim is not a platform builder that happens to have chat.

> Jasim is a general intelligent runtime that can answer, act directly, run long-lived workflows, or create and evolve persistent Smart Bubbles when persistence is genuinely useful.

> Selling does not require a platform.

> Advertising does not require a platform.

> Buying does not require a platform.

> Hiring does not require a platform.

> Monitoring does not require a platform.

> A persistent Smart Bubble is one powerful runtime form among several, not the mandatory path for every goal.

تابع التنفيذ الآن من الـBaseline الحالي ولا تعِد بناء الأجزاء التي ثبت أنها تعمل.
