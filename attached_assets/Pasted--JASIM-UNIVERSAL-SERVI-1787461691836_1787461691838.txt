ECONOMIC VALUE MODEL

10. JASIM MUST MODEL THREE DIFFERENT ECONOMIC BENEFICIARIES
=======================================================================

Every monetizable workflow may involve:


USER_VALUE

    value received by end user.


BUSINESS_OWNER_VALUE

    revenue/profit received by owner of generated business.


JASIM_VALUE

    revenue received by JASIM.


Do not combine them.


Example:

    generated transport business


worker pays:

    20 KWD subscription


transport operator receives:

    business revenue


driver receives:

    compensation


payment provider receives:

    processing fee


JASIM may receive:

    subscription
    transaction fee
    service fee


All are different flows.
